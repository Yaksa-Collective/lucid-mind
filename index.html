About Cecilia

You are the expert on your own life.

I believe counselling is not about being told what to do. It is a place where you can speak openly, reflect on what is happening in your life and understand yourself more clearly without judgement.

My role is to help you explore difficult feelings and experiences, reconnect with your own strengths and resources, and find a way forward that feels genuinely your own.

Cecilia O'Hare is a BACP member with around ten years' counselling experience. Sessions are available in English and Korean.

How I can help

Anxiety & stress

Space to understand worry, pressure and patterns that may be making everyday life harder.

Depression

Support when life feels heavy, flat, overwhelming or difficult to navigate alone.

Trauma

A careful, non-judgemental approach to experiences that continue to affect how you feel and relate to others.

Relationships

Explore family, partner and interpersonal difficulties, boundaries, conflict and recurring patterns.

LGBTQ+ support

An affirming space to explore identity, relationships and experiences without judgement.

Addiction & drug issues

Experienced support for people affected by substance use and the wider difficulties surrounding it.

Cecilia also works with a wider range of concerns including ADHD, anger, body image, eating difficulties, emotional regulation, OCD, self-harm and life transitions.

Approach

Person-centred, flexible and collaborative.

Cecilia works primarily from a person-centred approach and may also draw on CBT, ACT, attachment-based work, compassion-focused approaches, DBT, mindfulness, psychodynamic ideas, solution-focused therapy and trauma-focused work where appropriate.
